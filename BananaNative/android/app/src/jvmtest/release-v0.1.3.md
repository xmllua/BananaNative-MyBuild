## Banana Native v0.1.3

Install over v0.1.2: saves, packs and settings are kept.

- **`040074A01BF12000`: 30 fps on Turnip at Phone 1080p (was about 20; #5, #16).** The upscaler shader is rewritten for Adreno and the GPU clock no longer drops on Turnip. Phone 1080p is the default again; if you set Phone 720p for v0.1.2, switch back in Game settings › Game resolution. 60 fps is a later step.
- **One shader update after installing.** The app does it by itself when opened (112 shaders for `040074A01BF12000`, 175 for `0400C3F00006E000`); the game can start once its card says **Ready**. The update pauses while the phone is too hot and goes on once it cools.
- The build tag no longer shows the build time.

Needs Android 13+, a Snapdragon (Adreno) phone with 12 GB of RAM or more, and your own dump. Experimental: back up your saves. Download only from this page. No games, keys or shaders are included. GPL-3.0-or-later.
