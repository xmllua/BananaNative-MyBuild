# Videos and device reports

Back to the [main page](../../.github/README.md).

Videos are welcome. To make yours useful to viewers:

- Say the app version (for example v0.2.0), the phone, its chip and RAM, and the GPU driver.
- Turn on the performance overlay so the frame rate is visible, and say whether frame generation is on.
- Mention the [known problems](../../.github/README.md#games) of the game you show, so viewers know which ones are
  already known.
- Link the project's [GitHub page](https://github.com/moceure/BananaNative) for downloads: releases are only published
  there, and copies elsewhere may be modified.
- Please don't show or link game dumps, keys or firmware. The same goes for Issues and Discussions: posts that share,
  offer or ask for game files, keys or links are edited or removed
  ([rules](https://github.com/moceure/BananaNative/discussions/23)).

Post your video or your "it runs on my phone" report in
[Discussions](https://github.com/moceure/BananaNative/discussions): every phone other than the tested one helps, and
good videos get linked on the main page.

When something goes wrong, open an [issue](https://github.com/moceure/BananaNative/issues/new/choose) with the form
(phone, chip and RAM, GPU driver, game version, and the log from a game's ⋯ menu › **Share log**).
