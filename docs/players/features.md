# Features

Back to the [main page](../../.github/README.md).

- **Frame generation (LSFG):** Lossless Scaling's frame generation is built in: 2x, 3x or 4x, or a target of 60 / 90 /
  120 fps, with some added latency. It needs your own `Lossless.dll` (put it in `Download/` or pick it in Settings ›
  Frame generation).
- **Fold-aware layout:** separate layouts for the outer and inner screen, switched live when you fold or unfold, with
  no restart. Move the picture up or down and place the controls where you want them; on a square inner screen the
  picture can sit at the top with the controls below it.
- **Controls and settings:** on-screen controls with a layout editor, gamepad support, a frame-rate cap (the game's
  own, 60, 30, 20, 15), the game resolution (phone 1080p or the game's own), pause on return, and a performance overlay
  (fps, GPU time, throttling, battery, RAM).
- **GPU drivers:** Turnip driver downloads in the app.
- **Saves and packs:** saves and packs live in the app's storage (uninstalling asks whether to keep them). Save backups
  go to `Switch2/save-backups/<title ID>`; saves from 0.1.0 are copied in once. A game's pack can be exported and
  imported as one `.zip`, and a pack made on a computer can be imported with **Import pack…**
  ([Packs and app updates](../guides/packs-and-updates.md)).
- **Prepare on a computer or on the phone:** a window for Windows and the Mac, or the phone by itself
  ([Getting started](../guides/getting-started.md), [PC or Mac](../guides/pc-mac-packing.md)).
- **Launching from frontends** such as ES-DE, Daijisho, Beacon or Pegasus, with `.nup` files.
- **A text box** for games that ask you to type.
- **Updates in the app** (from v0.1.4): when a newer release is out, the app offers it at start (Update or Later),
  downloads it from the project's release and installs it after you confirm. Settings › **Updates** › **Check for
  updates** checks at any time.
- **Readiness check** (Settings): what your phone is missing for a game, with a button to fix most items.
